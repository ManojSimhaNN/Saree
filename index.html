About

Welcome to Dhanush Silk and Sarees, where tradition meets craftsmanship!

Founded by a family of weavers, we take immense pride in our rich heritage and commitment to quality. With our own handloom and power loom facilities, we create exquisite sarees that reflect the artistry and passion of our skilled artisans.

We have two branch one in Kuduru, a town where the majority of the population relies on weaving, is a testament to our commitment to preserving this age-old craft. Our second branch in Bangalore allows us to reach a wider audience, sharing the elegance and richness of our handcrafted sarees.

At Dhanush, each saree is crafted with love and attention to detail, ensuring that you receive not just a garment, but a piece of heritage that tells a story. Join us in celebrating the timeless beauty of sarees that reflect our culture and passion.
